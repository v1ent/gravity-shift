Rocket jump: fire a rocket at your feet and jump right after — it leaves the tube slowly. Blasts throw airborne fighters almost twice as hard and hurt them less; your own rockets do a third of the damage to you.

Kick a magenta panel on the outer walls and the whole hall turns so that wall becomes the floor. Everyone else falls, and so does the furniture. Bots only see what is in front of them (the cone and their reaction time depend on the skill level), need a moment to react when they spot you, and turn toward gunfire they hear. White spiders crawl out from time to time — on floors, walls and ceilings — and go for whoever is closest. Red barrels: 1 hit = 8 s fuse, 2 = on fire (3 s), 3 = boom. First to 15 frags wins.
