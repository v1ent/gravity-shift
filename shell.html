TUTORIAL // HOW TO PLAY

ESC in any room opens the menu — restart the room, pick another one, or go back to the main menu.

MOUSELook / aim (click the game to capture the mouse)
W A S DWalk · double-tap W / S / A / D to dodge: dive-roll forward, back, left, right (costs stamina)
SHIFTSprint (stamina) · vaults low walls and boxes (or climbs onto long ones) · you can shoot on the run (but not while aiming)
SPACEJump · from a sprint it's a hands-first dive · hold to reach for ledges
C (hold)Crouch · SHIFT+C = slide (slide into someone to kick them; slide over pickups to grab them)
C at low coverFacing a low wall or box (or sliding into one): take cover — you stay down until you press C again or walk away · hold right mouse to rise and shoot, let go to duck
LMBFire the selected weapon
RMB (hold)Aim: the view zooms, shots are tighter, you walk instead of running
MMBFire a rocket from the wrist launcher, whatever is in your hands (the standard one holds one rocket and recharges by itself)
WHEEL / Q · 1–41 pistol · 2 launcher (left click fires it; if it's still recharging you switch to the pistol and shoot) · 3 shotgun · 4 rifle — shotguns, rifles and the big 3-rocket launcher lie around the arena; a fighter who dies drops the one in their hands
EKick — knocks fighters down, sends boxes and spiders flying · at a magenta gravity panel the arena turns 90°
TWave — offer an alliance. Someone who stands in front of you and presses T too joins you
R / ESCReload / pause

Rockets (middle mouse, or slot 2): slow, then accelerating. A direct hit kills outright, and the blast hurts anyone near it — aim at the floor or a wall beside your target. Fighters in the air take little damage but get thrown hard. Rocket jump: fire at your feet — the blast always launches you, but on the ground it costs 60% of your health; jump just before it explodes and you fly higher for only 15–20%. Near the top you float for a moment. The tutorial teaches it.

Kick a magenta panel on the outer walls and the whole hall turns so that wall becomes the floor. Everyone else falls, and so does the furniture. Bots only see what is in front of them (the cone and their reaction time depend on the skill level), need a moment to react when they spot you, and turn toward gunfire they hear. White spiders crawl out from time to time — on floors, walls and ceilings — and go for whoever is closest. Red exploding boxes: 1 hit = 8 s fuse, 2 = on fire (3 s), 3 = boom. First to 15 frags wins — set 10 to 50 in Arena settings.

Alliances: press T to wave. A fighter who comes to stand in front of you and presses T too bows with you, and you take their colour: from then on you're a team until the round ends. Bots stop targeting you (friendly fire still hurts — a teamkill costs a frag), the team's score is its members' frags averaged, and if the team wins you all win. Bots offer and accept alliances too, and like to gang up on whoever is winning. There must always be two sides: the last lone fighter can't be absorbed.
